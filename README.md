# Robert Platt Lab Group

Research group for Dr. Robert Platt, a Professor in the departments of Pediatrics and of Epidemiology, Biostatistics, and Occupational Health (EBOH) at McGill University.
He is a Senior Investigator at the Research Institute of the McGill University Health Centre and the Lady Davis Institute of the Jewish General Hospital, Investigator at the McGill Pharmacoepidemiology Research Unit, holds the Albert Boehringer I endowed chair in Pharmacoepidemiology, and is the Executive Co-Lead and leader of the Methods team of the Canadian Network for Observational Drug Effect Studies (CNODES).

This organization hosts the group's research code to support transparency and reproducibility in scientific research.
